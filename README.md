# Texture Wall — fur demo

A browser demo of pseudo-haptic fur. You stroke a virtual fur patch with the mouse or trackpad; the fur bends, parts and springs back under the hand, and a small control/display mismatch makes stroking with the grain feel lighter than stroking against it. No physical force is applied: the effect is a visual and audio illusion only.

**Try it:** open the demo page (GitHub Pages) in a recent desktop Chrome, Firefox, Safari or Edge. It needs WebGL 2 and takes a few seconds to load.

## How to use

- **Press and drag** on the fur to stroke it. Left to right is with the grain.
- **Coat** menu (top right) switches between the cat and dog coats.
- Hovering just above the fur lets the fingertips brush the hair tips.
- **S** opens the study protocol: enter a participant code, then perform 12 short strokes (press on the dot, stroke to the right edge without releasing) and answer four rating questions after each. At the end the ratings are offered as a CSV download. Nothing is sent anywhere; the file stays on your computer and contains no personal data.
- **Esc** leaves the study protocol.

Sound is part of the illusion, so keep the volume on: slow gentle strokes give a soft warm brush, faster strokes a lighter, airier one.

## About

Research prototype by Massey Vo for a master's application on adaptive pseudo-haptics for affective touch. The study protocol measures how strongly people perceive a change in stroking resistance produced purely by visual and audio cues, and how it shifts pleasantness and calm.
